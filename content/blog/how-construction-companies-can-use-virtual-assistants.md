Construction is hands-on work, but running a construction business involves a mountain of paperwork. Estimates, bids, permits, insurance certificates, change orders, schedules and invoices all need managing, often by owners and project managers who would rather be on site.

Remote assistants can't pour concrete, but they can take a great deal of office admin off your plate. Here's how.

## The admin problem in construction

Many construction businesses run lean offices. The owner or a project manager handles calls, estimates and paperwork in between site visits, often in the evening. As the business grows, that model breaks:

- Bids go out late or not at all
- Subcontractor schedules clash
- Insurance and compliance documents expire unnoticed
- Clients chase for updates
- Invoices and progress claims are delayed, hurting cash flow

## Tasks a remote assistant can handle

### Document control

- Organizing drawings, specifications and project documents in your system
- Maintaining version control and naming conventions
- Distributing updated documents to the right people
- Keeping a register of RFIs, submittals and change orders

### Bids and estimates admin

- Tracking tender opportunities and deadlines
- Assembling bid packages from your inputs
- Formatting estimates and proposals
- Following up on submitted quotes

*Technical estimating (quantities, pricing and engineering judgment) stays with your team.*

### Scheduling and coordination

- Booking subcontractors and confirming dates
- Coordinating deliveries with suppliers
- Scheduling inspections
- Sending reminders before key milestones

### Compliance tracking

- Tracking insurance certificates, licenses and certifications for subcontractors
- Flagging upcoming expiries
- Maintaining safety documentation registers you specify

### Client communication

- Sending regular progress updates with photos from site
- Answering routine questions
- Scheduling client meetings and walkthroughs

### Finance administration

- Preparing invoices and progress claims from your figures
- Logging supplier bills
- Following up on overdue payments
- Preparing documents for your accountant

### Front office

- Answering calls and capturing new enquiries
- Booking estimate visits
- Responding to quote requests

## Construction software assistants commonly work in

Depending on your business: Procore, Buildertrend, Fieldwire, Jobber, ServiceTitan, QuickBooks Online, Xero, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. Your assistant should have their own user account with appropriate permissions.

## Setting it up

1. **List your recurring office tasks** and estimate the time each takes.
2. **Start with document control and scheduling:** they deliver quick, visible wins.
3. **Create simple checklists** for each process.
4. **Agree communication routines:** a morning schedule check, an end-of-day update.
5. **Define escalation rules** for anything safety-related or urgent.

## What stays with your team

- Site supervision and safety decisions
- Technical and engineering judgments
- Pricing decisions and contract negotiations
- Client relationships at key moments

## Time zones and construction

Construction days start early. If your assistant is in a different time zone, plan the day's schedule confirmations the evening before and use the overlap window for urgent coordination. Assistants in Nairobi, for instance, overlap well with UK and Australian working hours and US mornings.

## Putting it together

A remote assistant can transform the office side of a construction business: faster bids, organized documents, fewer scheduling clashes, better client communication and quicker payments. Keep the technical and site work with your team, and let someone else handle the paperwork.
