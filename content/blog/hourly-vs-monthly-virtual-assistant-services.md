**Choose hourly virtual assistant support if your workload is irregular, project-based or you're testing delegation for the first time. Choose a monthly package if you have recurring tasks every week and want a consistent assistant and predictable costs.** Many businesses start hourly and move to a monthly package once they know how much they can delegate.

Here's how the two models compare, and the questions to ask before you decide.

## What is hourly virtual assistant support?

With hourly support, you pay for the time your assistant actually spends on your work. Tasks are requested as they come up, time is tracked, and you're billed for the hours used, usually monthly.

**Works well for:**

- One-off projects, such as cleaning up a CRM or uploading a product catalogue
- Overflow help during busy seasons
- Occasional specialist tasks
- Businesses trying a VA for the first time

## What is a monthly virtual assistant package?

With a monthly package, you agree a level of support in advance, often a set number of hours or a defined scope, for a fixed monthly fee. Your assistant works on your recurring tasks every week and gets to know your business well.

**Works well for:**

- Daily or weekly tasks like inbox, calendar and customer emails
- Ongoing store, CRM or social media operations
- Businesses that want the same assistant over time
- Owners who prefer predictable monthly costs

## Hourly vs monthly at a glance

| Factor | Hourly support | Monthly package |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Variable or project work | Steady, recurring work |
| Cost predictability | Varies with usage | Fixed monthly amount |
| Flexibility | Very flexible | Adjustable at review points |
| Continuity | May vary by provider | Usually a consistent assistant |
| Planning | Request tasks as needed | Regular routine and priorities |
| Admin for you | Review hours each month | Review outcomes each month |

## Questions to ask before you choose

1. **How many hours a week do I actually need?** Track your delegable tasks for a week first.
2. **Is the work steady or seasonal?** Steady work favours a package; seasonal work favours hourly or a package with top-ups.
3. **Do I want the same person every time?** If continuity matters, ask how the provider handles it in each model.
4. **What happens to unused hours?** Some providers roll them over; others don't. Get it in writing.
5. **How are extra hours billed?** Check the rate for hours beyond a package.
6. **What's the notice period?** Make sure you can adjust or end the arrangement on reasonable terms.
7. **Which currency will I be billed in?** Prices should be set in your currency, not converted on the fly.

## Common mistakes

- **Buying a big package too soon.** Start smaller and scale once delegation is working.
- **Staying hourly when the work is steady.** You may miss out on continuity and a smoother routine.
- **Comparing headline rates only.** Look at what's included: matching, onboarding, oversight and cover.
