Choosing an international virtual assistant agency is an important decision. The right partner can give you dependable support for years. The wrong one can cost you time, money and peace of mind.

This checklist covers what to look for, and the red flags to avoid.

## 1. Transparency about who they are

A trustworthy agency is clear about:

- **Where it's based** and where assistants work from
- **Who owns and runs it**
- **How long it has operated:** and is honest if it's new
- **What it does well:** and what it doesn't do

Be cautious of agencies that are vague about their location or imply local offices they don't have.

## 2. How they select assistants

Ask how the agency recruits and assesses talent:

- How do they test written and spoken communication?
- Do they assess skills relevant to your tasks?
- Do they check references or work history?
- How do they evaluate reliability and working setup?
- Can you meet your assistant before starting?

## 3. Matching and onboarding

A good agency doesn't just assign whoever is available. Look for:

- A discovery process to understand your needs
- Matching based on skills, tools and communication style
- A structured onboarding plan
- Help documenting your processes

## 4. Communication and management

- Who is your point of contact besides the assistant?
- How are check-ins and reviews handled?
- What reporting do you receive?
- How are issues escalated and resolved?

## 5. Time-zone fit

Ask exactly which hours your assistant will work and how much overlap you'll have. A good agency will be honest about what's practical rather than promising round-the-clock coverage it can't deliver.

## 6. Security and data protection

This is non-negotiable. Ask:

- Will you sign an NDA?
- How do assistants access client systems: individual accounts or shared logins?
- Do you use password managers?
- What device and network security do assistants follow?
- How is access removed when an engagement ends?
- Which data protection laws do you consider, and can you sign a data processing agreement if needed?

Be cautious of certification claims that can't be verified. Ask to see evidence of any certification an agency says it holds.

## 7. AI use

AI tools are increasingly part of assistant work. Ask:

- Which AI tools do assistants use?
- Do you get to approve their use?
- How is confidential data protected?
- Is all AI-assisted output reviewed by a person?

## 8. Pricing clarity

Look for:

- Clear, written quotes
- Transparent explanation of what's included
- Clarity on hours, overtime and how time is tracked
- Upfront information on setup fees, minimum terms and notice periods
- Billing in a currency that suits you

## 9. Contracts and terms

Your agreement should cover:

- Scope of services
- Working hours and availability
- Fees and payment terms
- Confidentiality and data protection
- Ownership of work product
- Term, notice and termination
- What happens if the assistant is unavailable

## 10. Continuity

Assistants sometimes move on. Ask:

- What happens if my assistant leaves or is sick?
- How is knowledge transferred?
- How quickly can a replacement be onboarded?

Agencies that document processes well make continuity much smoother.

## 11. Honest marketing

Pay attention to how the agency presents itself:

- Are testimonials verifiable, with real names and companies (with permission)?
- Are claims specific and believable, or vague guarantees about revenue and growth?
- Do statistics have sources?

An agency that's honest in its marketing is more likely to be honest in its service.

## Red flags

- Guaranteed results ("double your revenue")
- Reviews or testimonials that can't be verified
- Pressure to sign quickly or commit to long terms upfront
- Requests for owner-level access or payment details on day one
- Vague answers about who does the work
- Unverifiable certification claims
- No written agreement

## A short evaluation process

1. Shortlist two or three agencies.
2. Hold discovery calls and ask the questions above.
3. Compare proposals, not just price, but what's included.
4. Meet the proposed assistant.
5. Start with a defined scope and review after the first month.

## Final thoughts

The best international VA agencies are transparent, careful about talent, strong communicators, serious about security, clear on pricing and honest in their marketing. Use this checklist, ask direct questions and trust your instincts.
