UK small businesses face a familiar squeeze: rising costs, tight margins and owners who end up doing everything themselves. A virtual assistant, or "virtual PA," as many in the UK say, can be a practical way to get capable help without the commitment of a full-time hire.

This guide covers what UK small businesses typically delegate, how to choose between UK-based and overseas assistants, and the practical considerations for getting started.

## Virtual assistant or virtual PA?

In the UK, the terms are often used interchangeably. Broadly:

- A **virtual PA** usually supports one director or business owner closely: diary, inbox, travel and correspondence.
- A **virtual assistant** may support the business more widely: customer enquiries, admin, marketing or operations.

What matters most is defining the work clearly, whatever you call the role.

## What UK small businesses delegate

### Admin and PA support

- Inbox and diary management
- Correspondence and document preparation
- Travel bookings and itineraries
- Meeting notes and follow-ups

### Customer service

- Responding to email and website enquiries
- Live chat during business hours
- Order and booking enquiries
- Review requests and responses

### Finance administration

- Raising invoices
- Chasing overdue payments politely
- Receipt capture in Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent
- Preparing documents for your accountant

*These are admin tasks; VAT returns, tax and accounting advice should stay with your accountant.*

### Marketing

- Social media scheduling
- Newsletter formatting
- Blog uploads and website updates
- Canva graphics

### Operations

- Supplier communication
- Updating spreadsheets and systems
- Process documentation

## UK-based or overseas assistant?

### UK-based VAs

**Pros:** Same time zone, local knowledge, easy in-person meetings if needed.

**Considerations:** Typically higher rates, reflecting UK living costs.

### Overseas VAs

**Pros:** Often more cost-effective; access to a wide talent pool.

**Considerations:** Time zones, communication style and data protection need planning.

### Why Kenya is a natural fit for UK businesses

Kenya is just **two hours ahead of the UK in summer and three hours ahead in winter**. That means an assistant in Nairobi can easily work hours covering your entire business day. English is an official language in Kenya, and there are long-standing business ties between the two countries.

For many UK small businesses, this offers a useful middle ground: real-time collaboration like a UK assistant, with the cost-effectiveness of remote support.

## UK GDPR and data protection

If your assistant handles personal data (customer details, employee information, leads) you have responsibilities under the **UK GDPR** and the **Data Protection Act 2018**. Key points:

- Put a written agreement in place covering how data is handled (often called a data processing agreement).
- Limit access to what's needed.
- If personal data is transferred outside the UK, check that appropriate safeguards are in place.
- Make sure your privacy notice reflects how you use service providers.

The [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/) publishes practical guidance for small businesses. This article isn't legal advice, so check specifics with your adviser.

## Electronic marketing rules

If your assistant helps with email or SMS marketing, follow the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) (including consent and opt-out rules).

## How to get started

1. **List your recurring tasks** for a week.
2. **Choose a starting bundle:** inbox, diary and customer enquiries are popular.
3. **Decide on hours:** part-time is a common starting point.
4. **Agree communication channels:** email, Slack, Teams or WhatsApp.
5. **Set up secure access** with individual logins and a password manager.
6. **Plan the first month** with regular check-ins.

## A typical week with a virtual PA

- **Daily:** Inbox triage, diary updates, customer replies
- **Monday:** Weekly priorities agreed on a short call
- **Wednesday:** Invoices raised and payment chasers sent
- **Thursday:** Social posts scheduled for the following week
- **Friday:** End-of-week summary and next week's diary review
