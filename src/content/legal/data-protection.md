This page explains how **Bravura VA** approaches data protection and confidentiality when supporting client businesses. It complements our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/), which covers personal data collected through this website.

## Our role

When we provide services, we usually act on our client's instructions in relation to the client's data (in many jurisdictions, as a "processor" or "service provider"). Our client remains responsible for deciding why and how their data is used. Specific responsibilities are set out in each client agreement, and we can enter into a data processing agreement where required.

## Laws we consider

We are based in Kenya and take into account the **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019**. Depending on our clients' locations and sectors, client data may also be subject to laws such as the **UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018**, the **Australian Privacy Act 1988**, and US federal and state laws. We discuss relevant requirements with each client before work begins.

**We do not claim any certification** (such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA or "GDPR certification"). Where a client's sector has specialist obligations (for example, healthcare, legal or financial services) those obligations must be assessed and agreed before any work begins.

## Our practices

- **Confidentiality:** confidentiality expectations agreed in writing; NDAs signed on request.
- **Least-privilege access:** assistants receive only the access needed for agreed tasks, ideally through individual user accounts.
- **No shared passwords:** we recommend password managers with shared vaults rather than sending credentials by email or chat.
- **Work in your systems:** client data stays in client systems wherever possible, and is not copied to personal devices or unapproved tools.
- **Responsible AI use:** confidential or personal data is not entered into AI tools unless the client has approved that use.
- **Access removal:** access is reviewed regularly and removed promptly when no longer required.
- **Incident reporting:** if we become aware of a security incident affecting client data, we will notify the client without undue delay and cooperate in the response.
