This Cookie Policy explains how **Bravura VA** uses cookies and similar technologies on **bravura.co.ke**.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, which websites use to remember settings.

## How we use them

### Essential (always on)

These are needed for the website to work and cannot be switched off:

- **Cookie consent preference:** remembers your choice (stored in your browser's local storage as `bva-consent`).
- **Form and session storage:** temporarily remembers tasks you select in our delegation checklist so they can be added to the enquiry form (session storage, cleared when you close the tab).
- **Security:** measures that protect forms from spam.

### Analytics (optional)

If you choose **Accept all**, and once analytics is configured, we may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website (for example, which pages are visited). These cookies are **not** loaded unless you consent. **[EDIT: confirm the analytics provider and cookie names used once configured.]**

### Third-party embeds

If you use our booking tool, our booking provider (for example, Calendly) may set its own cookies within the embedded scheduler. Please refer to that provider's cookie policy.

## Managing your choices

You can change your preferences at any time using the **Cookie Settings** link in the website footer, or by clearing your browser's site data. Most browsers also let you block cookies through their settings.
